Plan your UW degree, quarter by quarter.

Start from your DARS audit so your AP, transfer and current courses are already in place. Then add the courses you plan to take, and the planner shows the first quarter where you meet every requirement.

Where's DARS? In MyPlan, click Degree Audit (DARS), run or refresh your audit, then copy the whole audit page.

Sign in to save — nothing is kept if you close the tab or refresh without an account. Use Export to back up your plans or move them to another device.

Requirement numbers follow the UW General Catalog and department websites for AY 2026-27. GPA, minimum grade, reasoning (RSN) and foreign language requirements aren't tracked. This is an unofficial student tool and is not affiliated with the University of Washington or any of its departments. Always confirm with your DARS audit and your adviser.
