Husky Degree Planner

Plans are saved in this browser.

Plan your UW CS degree, quarter by quarter

Start from your DARS audit so your AP, transfer and current courses are already in place. Then add the courses you plan to take, and the planner shows the first quarter where you meet every requirement.

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Use Export to back up your plans or move them to another device.

Requirement numbers follow the UW General Catalog for the BS in Computer Science (College of Arts & Sciences gen-ed rules, catalog year AU 26). This is an unofficial student tool and is not affiliated with the University of Washington or the Allen School. Always confirm with your DARS audit and your adviser.
